    What is real and what is simulated

    Real gateway code: identity and application allowlist, content security, routing and ranking, policy, budget reservations, response cache, failover and circuit breaker, usage ledger, audit trail.
    Simulated: the model provider (deterministic token counts, a scripted outage) and the traffic itself. Prices come from the repository’s fixture table, so dollar figures show how the mechanics work, not billed spend or forecast savings.
